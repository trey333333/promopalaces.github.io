## 1. How to Promote a Small Business on a $500 Budget

- Search intent: practical low-budget promotion plan for a new or local small business.
- Reader: owner with a limited, self-funded first marketing budget.
- Promise: a prioritization framework, not a guaranteed-results plan.
- Outline: define a customer and offer; reserve essential operating costs; choose one owned channel; test one local or relationship-based tactic; set a stop condition; review results.
- Evidence needed: current local-marketing cost ranges from primary providers or owner records; no generic ROI claim.
- Affiliate rule: cite a partner only when a verified, approved option fits a named step. Do not claim a discount, commission, or savings without evidence.
