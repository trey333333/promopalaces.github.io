# PP-003 Owner-review application packets

## Status and boundary

These are preparation packets, not applications. Each named provider is a researched candidate only: it has no PromoPalaces affiliate ID, no tracking link, no owner approval, and no permission to appear on a public page. Sending any application requires a separate, active owner decision covering the `affiliate_applications` gate and the exact candidate scope. A later acceptance never authorizes tracking or publication.

Before an owner signs a decision, confirm the current provider agreement in the application flow. Public program terms can change after this research was recorded on 2026-10-08.

## Packet 1 — GetResponse (ARC-GETRESPONSE-001)

- Department: Email Marketing.
- Official application entry: <https://www.getresponse.com/affiliate-programs>.
- Public terms observed: Bronze pays 40% recurring for 12 months, rising to 50% after 50 sales and 60% after 100 sales in 12 months; the stated referral cookie is 90 days. The program is managed through PartnerStack.
- Material restrictions: accurate application information; no self-purchases or affiliate-funded accounts; no incentives/cashback; paid advertising and direct linking require prior written or email consent. Review the [program agreement](https://www.getresponse.com/legal/affiliate-program) before any submission.
- Proposed educational asset after acceptance: email marketing platform selection checklist, with workflow-fit questions rather than product-performance claims.
- Owner decision scope to review: submit one GetResponse/PartnerStack application only. It must exclude any tracking-link creation, promo-code request, paid promotion, publication, or customer-data sharing beyond the information deliberately submitted by the owner.

## Packet 2 — Hostinger (ARC-HOSTINGER-001)

- Department: Websites & Ecommerce.
- Official application entry: <https://www.hostinger.com/affiliates>.
- Public terms observed: the public program page says commission starts at 40% and increases with eligible sales volume; a localized official affiliate agreement describes up to 40% for specified products and a cookie stored up to 30 days.
- Material restrictions: account verification is required; do not use iframe or similar cookie-setting techniques without a real click; payout follows the money-back period. Confirm the governing jurisdiction-specific [affiliate agreement](https://www.hostinger.com/br/legal/contrato-de-programas-afiliados) and [payout support article](https://www.hostinger.com/support/1583263-how-are-affiliate-commissions-paid-at-hostinger/) before submission.
- Proposed educational asset after acceptance: website launch planning guide, with vendor-neutral domain, content, conversion, and maintenance questions.
- Owner decision scope to review: submit one Hostinger affiliate application only. No public recommendation, link, coupon, tracking configuration, or paid campaign is in scope.

## Packet 3 — Shopify (ARC-SHOPIFY-001)

- Department: Websites & Ecommerce.
- Official application entry: <https://www.shopify.com/affiliates>.
- Public terms observed: up to US$150 per qualified referral, varying by referral location; 30-day click tracking, with up to 400 days for a free-trial referral to become a Paid Trial and Full Price store. The program is managed through Impact.
- Material restrictions: Shopify prioritizes active sites, an established merchant audience, original commerce/entrepreneurship content, and relevant experience; not every application is approved. Commission requires a qualifying full-price plan. Review the [program help](https://help.shopify.com/en/affiliates) and [earnings guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/affiliates/earnings) at the time of application.
- Proposed educational asset after acceptance: ecommerce launch decision guide covering catalog, fulfillment, payments, support, and operating readiness.
- Owner decision scope to review: submit one Shopify application only. No public use of Shopify marks, tracking link, or comparative claim is permitted by this packet.

## Packet 4 — Semrush (ARC-SEMRUSH-001)

- Department: SEO & Advertising.
- Official application entry: <https://www.semrush.com/lp/affiliate-program/en/>.
- Public terms observed: the current public page lists US$100–US$300 per sale plus US$10 per free trial, with a 120-day last-click cookie. Program enrollment is through Impact.
- Material restrictions: the public page does not publish the complete traffic-source or paid-search rules. The owner must inspect the current Impact agreement before applying. Public Semrush materials differ in historical maximum-payout wording, so only the current offer shown to the owner may be used.
- Proposed educational asset after acceptance: an SEO research workflow guide about goals, search intent, and measurement—not rankings guarantees.
- Owner decision scope to review: submit one Semrush/Impact application only. It excludes public comparison pages, claims, link placement, tracking configuration, and paid promotion.

## Owner-supplied information checklist

Do not infer or fill these fields. The owner or explicitly authorized human should provide and submit only the information each application actually requests:

- Legal applicant identity, authorized contact, and business address.
- Tax and payout information, if requested.
- Active site URL and accurate traffic/audience/channel descriptions.
- Intended promotion methods and domains.
- Required legal attestations after reading the then-current provider and network terms.

## After a provider accepts

Acceptance is evidence for the `accepted` workflow stage only. Before any public placement, record the assigned identifier and exact link privately; review the agreement; verify attribution without a purchase; obtain active owner decisions for both `affiliate_tracking_changes` and `publishing`; complete disclosures and release checks; then add a hardened partner-registry entry through an approved task.
